## World Timesheet generation prompt

Built-in `image_gen`, `transparent_background: true`. The generated PNG is used unchanged.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: World Timesheet launcher watercolor vignette for a native Windows desktop application. Create one square RGBA PNG on a genuinely transparent background. Delicate hand-painted watercolor with pigment blooms, selective fine details and irregular soft edges. Subject: an ivory paper timetable sheet angled slightly, with five clearly spaced vertical columns of teal, blue and muted warm gold time blocks, accompanied by a small blue-and-ochre globe and one elegant small analog clock. Evoke comparing local times across cities and a printable schedule, not activity tracking. Cohesive clustered composition readable at 120 pixels, center 65 percent filled, airy watercolor washes dissolving to transparent alpha before all canvas edges. Ivory paper, indigo and turquoise ink, restrained ochre accents consistent with watercolor world clock illustrations. No words, letters, numerals, logos, rectangular background, opaque paper backdrop, drawn checkerboard or UI screenshot. Artwork only.
