# Launcher watercolor provenance

I commissioned these six decorative corner illustrations for WorkTrail on 2026-10-22, following the owner's art direction and approval to use the integrated OpenAI image generator instead of Midjourney. Each original PNG is 1254 × 1254 pixels with transparent and partially transparent alpha. I copied the generated files unchanged.

These are project-authored brand illustrations; see [TRADEMARKS.md](../../../TRADEMARKS.md). The MIT header applies to this provenance document, not to the brand artwork.

## Import verification

Before copying the staged originals into this asset directory, I ran Microsoft Windows Defender with `MpCmdRun.exe -Scan -ScanType 3 -File <staged-directory>`. The scan completed with exit code 0 and reported no threats. The hashes below identify those unchanged files. Match delicate hand-painted watercolor city illustrations: visible pigment blooms, soft watery edges, fine selective architectural-style brush detail, luminous restrained color, airy negative space. Keep the recognizable subject concentrated in the central 55 percent of the canvas, readable at thumbnail size. Around the subject, watercolor washes become progressively fainter in all directions and dissolve smoothly to fully transparent alpha well before every canvas edge. Preserve a few soft irregular pigment wisps. No opaque paper, no white background, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no rectangular scene, no badge, no frame, no lettering, no logo, no drop shadow. Give the painting gentle but sufficient contrast for use on both light and dark acrylic. The final output is the artwork only, not a button or app screenshot. Subject: A charming cluster of three overlapping analog world clocks at slightly different angles, two prominent faces and one smaller behind, each showing a different time. Ivory clock faces, fine dark hands, blue and turquoise metal rims with a few warm ochre accents. Recognizable clocks at the center, loose blue watercolor washes dissolving outward. Do not include letters, city names, numbers or a map.
