### Generation prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a production banner illustration for the About window of WorkTrail, a desktop activity history and time tracking app.
Primary request: a visual trail that suggests retracing a working day.
Scene/backdrop: dark ink navy #101526, subtle sculpted topographic contours and soft layered terrain.
Subject: one elegant lilac #AF9BFF trail winding across the scene, linking three restrained warm ivory waymarkers: a small stack of activity snapshot tiles, a document tile, and a simple clock without numerals. The trail should feel like a real route through a quiet landscape, while the waymarkers gently suggest past desktop work.
Style/medium: premium editorial illustration, matte paper and restrained dimensional relief, precise thin lines, soft studio illumination. Clean and contemporary, understated.
Composition/framing: very wide panoramic landscape, 3:1 aspect ratio. Leave the left 38 percent calm and visually quiet for a native UI title overlay. Place most terrain and waymarkers across the center and right; keep every important object within the middle 65 percent of the image height so a shallow banner crop stays coherent. Generous edges, no clutter.
Text: none. The product name will be rendered by the app.
Constraints: this is a standalone art asset, not a screenshot or UI mockup. No lettering, words, logo, watermark, frame, people, hiking equipment, neon glow, or old product branding. No VibeWare logo inside the artwork; the app will display the authentic logo separately.
```
